Add a staff member

The account works immediately. Give the staff member the username and password; at their first log-in the system makes them choose their own password.

Roles. A Teacher enters marks for the subjects you give them, and manages a class if you make them its class teacher (do both under “Edit / subjects”). An Administrator can do everything you can, including managing staff. Disable stops a log-in but keeps the account; Delete removes it for good (marks they entered are always kept).
