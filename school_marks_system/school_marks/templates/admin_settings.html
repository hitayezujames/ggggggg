How marks are calculated

A subject's mark = (Tests % × tests weight + Exam % × exam weight) of that subject's maximum. Example with Mathematics out of 60: tests 80 %, exam 90 %, weights 40/60 → 86 % of 60 = 51.6. TOTAL = all subject marks added; PERCENTAGE = total ÷ the maximums added; POSITION by percentage. Conduct is printed but not added to the total. For a report from tests only (no exam yet), set “Tests count for” to 100. Maximums of subjects are set under Levels & subjects.

Email to staff

When this is switched on, the system emails a staff member when their registration is received and approved, when an account is created for them, when their role changes, when they are given a subject or made class teacher, and when their password is reset. Administrators are emailed when someone registers. An email never contains a password.
